## 🔬 MITOCHONDRIAL & CELLULAR HEALTH PEPTIDES

### 1️⃣ MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA-c)

**🧪 What it is:** Mitochondrial-derived peptide that regulates metabolism and mitochondrial function

**✨ Claimed Benefits:**
- ⚡ Improved insulin sensitivity
- 🏭 Enhanced mitochondrial biogenesis
- 🏃 Better exercise performance
- 📊 Metabolic regulation
- ⏰ Age-related decline reversal

**📊 Research Status:** Early human trials, mostly animal studies show promise

**💉 Dosing:** 5-15mg, 2-3x/week subcutaneous injection

**💰 Cost:** $150-300/month

**⏱️ Time Investment:** 5 minutes, 2-3x/week for injection

**🆚 vs. Natural Methods:**
- 🏃 **Exercise (Zone 2 cardio)** achieves mitochondrial biogenesis naturally - FREE, 4-5 hours/week
- ⏰ **Time-restricted eating** improves insulin sensitivity - FREE, no extra time
- ✅ **Verdict:** MOTS-c might accelerate results by 2-3 months, but exercise provides same endpoint with broader health benefits

---

### 2️⃣ Humanin & HNG (Humanin G)

**🧪 What it is:** Mitochondrial-encoded peptide with neuroprotective and cytoprotective properties

**✨ Claimed Benefits:**
- 🛡️ Cellular protection from oxidative stress
- 🧠 Neuroprotection
- ⏰ Anti-aging effects at cellular level
- 🍬 Improved glucose metabolism
- ❤️ Cardiovascular protection

**📊 Research Status:** Promising animal studies, limited human data

**💉 Dosing:** 2-4mg/day, subcutaneous

**💰 Cost:** $200-400/month

**⏱️ Time Investment:** Daily injections, 5 min/day

**🆚 vs. Natural Methods:**
- 🍵 **Polyphenols** (green tea, berries, turmeric) activate similar protective pathways - $10-20/month
- 🏃 **Exercise + caloric restriction** naturally increase endogenous humanin - FREE
- ✅ **Verdict:** Expensive with unclear human benefit. Natural methods produce humanin endogenously

---

### 3️⃣ Epithalon (Epitalon)

**🧪 What it is:** Synthetic peptide that may influence telomerase activity

**✨ Claimed Benefits:**
- 🧬 Telomere lengthening
- 🌙 Circadian rhythm regulation
- 😴 Melatonin production
- ⏰ Anti-aging at cellular level
- 💤 Improved sleep

**📊 Research Status:** Mostly Russian studies, limited Western peer review, controversial

**💉 Dosing:** 10-20 day cycles, 5-10mg/day

**💰 Cost:** $100-200/cycle, 2-4 cycles/year = $200-800/year

**⏱️ Time Investment:** 10-20 days of daily injections

**🆚 vs. Natural Methods:**
- 😴 **Sleep optimization + stress reduction** naturally preserve telomeres - FREE
- 🧘 **Exercise, meditation, plant-based diet** all shown to maintain telomere length - FREE to minimal cost
- ✅ **Verdict:** Questionable efficacy, natural methods have stronger evidence for telomere preservation

### 5️⃣ NAD+ Precursors (NMN, NR) - Technically not peptides but often grouped

**🧪 What it is:** Precursors that boost NAD+ levels (critical coenzyme for cellular energy)

**✨ Claimed Benefits:**
- 🏭 Enhanced mitochondrial function
- ⚡ Improved cellular energy
- 🧬 DNA repair support
- 🧪 Sirtuin activation (longevity pathways)
- 🧠 Cognitive enhancement

**📊 Research Status:** Growing human evidence, David Sinclair's research popularized

**💊 Dosing:** NMN: 250-1000mg/day oral; NR: 300-1000mg/day

**💰 Cost:** $30-100/month depending on dose

**⏱️ Time Investment:** Daily pill, 30 seconds

**🆚 vs. Natural Methods:**
- 🏃 **Exercise** naturally boosts NAD+ - FREE
- ⏰ **Fasting** increases NAD+/NADH ratio - FREE
- 🥜 **Niacin-rich foods** (mushrooms, peanuts, green peas) support NAD+ - $5-10/month
- ✅ **Verdict:** More accessible than peptides, some evidence, but lifestyle factors strongly influence NAD+ naturally. Cost-effective option if budget allows, but not necessary with proper lifestyle
